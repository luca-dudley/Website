Consultancy FAQ

Questions About Custom Builds

Do you build outside of ClickUp?

Absolutely. ClickUp is a common starting point, but we specialise in choosing the right tool for the job — Power BI, Supabase, Make.com, custom APIs, and more.

How does fixed-scope project pricing work?

After the discovery call we define a clear, milestone-based scope with a fixed price for each milestone — no open-ended hourly billing.

Who owns the intellectual property and data at handover?

You do — completely. At live handover you receive full ownership of your data, dashboards, automations, and workspace.
