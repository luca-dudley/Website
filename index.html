FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the video streaming library work?

Every plan streams our full catalog of AI-generated safety training videos directly in the browser. Videos include multiple language audio tracks so each worker can watch in their preferred language during training sessions.

What is the difference between Basic and Essential plans?

The Basic plan gives you full access to the streaming video catalog and 1 admin seat. Whereas, the Essential plan adds the Training Records Directory & Dashboard, worker compliance logging via single or group sign-off mode, and 3 additional management sub-accounts.

How do group training session sign-offs work?

On the Essential tier and above, you play a training video for the crew, then capture a single group sign-off via an online form. Each attendee's name and ID number is logged for the session and the record lands in your Training Records Dashboard instantly apon submission of the form.

What if my business needs custom videos or bespoke operational tools?

We can build a solution for virtually any operational challenge. If you require custom, site-specific safety videos, we can produce them for your farm or facility and host them directly inside your private Vault catalog. For broader workflow bottlenecks, custom automations, or specialized software, simply contact us to map out a tailored project plan—there is no operational task we can't attempt to solve.
